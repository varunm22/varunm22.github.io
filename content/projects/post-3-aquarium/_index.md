This virtual aquarium is meant to simulate various aspects of my real aquarium I kept for a few years. My intention was to encode logic with relatively simple rules that with enough nesting would create realistic behavior and emergent complexity. As examples: 

- Fish show loose shoaling behavior where if not actively foraging, they tend to follow the group, especially when individuals move quickly
- Overfeeding the aquarium causes snail populations to explode. Stopping overfeeding causes many of the snails to eventually die off, with empty shells gathering in the corners of the tank.
